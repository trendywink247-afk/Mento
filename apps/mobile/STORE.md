## Full Description (App Store + Play Store — 4000 chars max)

```
A safe place. A place for guidance. A place to be yourself.

Mento is where someone who has walked a hard path turns around and holds a light for the person still walking it. We start with UPSC because the people who have lived inside that crucible carry rare values — discipline, ethics, empathy, civic depth — and the world isn't using that wisdom well.

You don't need a coaching centre. You need someone who sat exactly where you are sitting now.

────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT MENTO IS
────────────────────────────────────────

• One-on-one anonymous chat with verified UPSC mentors
• Mentors who cleared Prelims (at least once) — real experience, real empathy
• A journal for your UPSC journey — personal, per-mentor, and subject-wise
• A safe space where you can be honest about where you really are

────────────────────────────────────────
WHAT MENTO IS NOT
────────────────────────────────────────

We are not coaching. Not content. Not a doubt-clearing service. Not therapy. Not a social network.

We are one human, anonymously, sitting with another.

────────────────────────────────────────
ANONYMITY — BUILT INTO THE ARCHITECTURE
────────────────────────────────────────

No real names. No photos. Your profile is a single letter on a colored tile. Your mentor's profile is the same. Nobody knows who you are. That is the point — honest conversation requires safety, and safety requires anonymity.

The only person who knows your real identity is you.

────────────────────────────────────────
KEY FEATURES
────────────────────────────────────────

MENTOR DISCOVERY
Browse verified mentors by Prelims/Mains/Interview stage, subject specialisation, language, and availability. Every profile shows their UPSC journey year by year — the attempts, the gaps, the eventual clarity. No fabrication. No marketing copy.

ANONYMOUS CHAT
Send a 160-character intro request to any mentor. If they accept, the conversation opens. WhatsApp-simple. A persistent safety banner reminds both sides: "Please avoid sharing personal details."

JOURNALS
Your UPSC journey deserves documentation. Personal journal. Subject-wise notes (Polity, History, Economy, all GS papers, Essay, Optional). Per-mentor shared journals — visible only to you and that mentor, editable only when you are both online at the same time.

1-ON-1 SESSIONS (coming in v1.1)
Book a private session with a mentor. Voice and video. Opt-in transcription that saves into your journal automatically.

────────────────────────────────────────
MENTOR VERIFICATION
────────────────────────────────────────

Every mentor submits their Mains hall ticket and journey documentation. Our team manually verifies each one. Verified mentors carry a purple tick. We do not rate humans with stars — only positive written reflections from mentees are surfaced.

────────────────────────────────────────
PRICING
────────────────────────────────────────

Basic (₹399/mo) — Anonymous chat, personal journals, mentor discovery, free intro requests.
Pro (₹599/mo) — Everything in Basic, plus broadcast requests and priority in mentor feed.
Max (₹999/mo) — Everything in Pro, plus 1-on-1 session credits and group session access.

First intro request is always free.

────────────────────────────────────────
A NOTE ON COMPETITION
────────────────────────────────────────

We don't rank mentors. We don't compare aspirants. We don't believe the UPSC journey needs more pressure added to it. Mento exists to reduce the weight, not add to it.

────────────────────────────────────────
PRIVACY & LEGAL
────────────────────────────────────────

Privacy Policy: https://mento.in/privacy
Terms of Use: https://mento.in/terms

We honour the struggle. We never promise outcomes.
```
